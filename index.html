The honest part: how posting actually works

        
No outside app can silently post straight into your eBay or Facebook seller account — the marketplaces don't allow it. The big cross-listers get around that by working through your own signed-in browser sessions, account by account. The Multimarket doesn't fake “posted” buttons or pretend otherwise.

        
What it does instead is the next-fastest honest thing: build the listing once, hand you a sized, copy-ready pack for each marketplace, walk you down the checklist, and track what's live where — including the part everyone forgets, pulling it all down when it sells.

The Sell Faster playbook

        
Inside the app, a Sell Faster tab turns these into checks against each listing. Here's the short version — the stuff that actually moves storage-unit and cleanout inventory:

      

      

        

          
Price the search, not the item

          
Check sold listings — not active ones — for your exact item before you price. Active listings show what sellers hope; sold listings show what buyers pay. Price at the sold median and turn on offers for anything sitting past two weeks.

        

        

          
Fill every field they give you

          
On eBay, complete item specifics (brand, model, era, material) are what search ranks on. The pack reminds you per marketplace — a half-filled listing is invisible no matter how good the item is.

        

        

          
Match the item to the room

          
Coins and collectibles → eBay first. Furniture and anything heavy → Facebook Marketplace and OfferUp, local pickup, no shipping headache. Clothing and streetwear → Poshmark and Depop. Vintage or handmade → Etsy, and only Etsy-legal items (20+ years old, handmade, or craft supplies).

        

        

          
First photo does the selling

          
Bright, straight-on, nothing else in frame. Mercari caps you at 12 photos, Facebook at 10, eBay allows 24 — the pack orders your best shots first so the tightest limit still gets the good ones.

        

        

          
Refresh stale listings

          
A listing untouched for 30 days sinks. End it and relist with a tightened title, or drop the price 5–10% and let the watchers get the alert. In the app, your board shows you what's gone stale.

        

        

          
Never sell it twice

          
The fastest way to burn a marketplace account is a second buyer paying for an item that's already gone. The sold → pull-down-everywhere checklist exists for exactly this — stamp each dead listing off as you kill it.

Straight answers

      

      

        Does The Multimarket post to eBay and Facebook for me automatically?
        
No — and be suspicious of anything that promises that casually. Marketplaces don't let an outside web app post silently into your seller account; legitimate cross-listers work through your own signed-in sessions. The Multimarket builds each listing once, hands you a copy-ready pack sized for each marketplace, walks you down a posting checklist, and tracks the live link for each one. It's minutes per item instead of retyping eight listings — without fake “posted” buttons.

      

      

        Which marketplaces does it cover?
        
Eight: eBay, Facebook Marketplace, Mercari, Poshmark, Etsy, Depop, OfferUp, and Craigslist. Each gets its own pack — title trimmed to that site's character limit (Mercari and Poshmark cap at 80, for example), its photo limit (eBay 24, Mercari 12, Facebook 10), its fee note, and a posting tip for that room.

      

      

        How does the profit calculator work?
        
Simple on purpose: your profit is your sale price minus the selling fee, minus shipping, minus what you paid for the item. You set the fee percentage to match wherever you're selling, and the fee comes straight out of your profit automatically — in the calculator above and on every sold record in the app. It's just your numbers, worked out before you price instead of after you're paid.

      

      

        What happens when an item sells?
        
You mark it sold on the marketplace where it sold. The Multimarket records the sale with the real fee and your profit after cost, then gives you a pull-down checklist for the other marketplaces so every copy of the listing comes down — that's how you avoid the classic double sale and the refund-and-apology that comes with it.

      

      

        What does it cost?
        
Early access is free while the public launch is being built. Nothing on this page charges you anything, and the fee calculator and pack preview above stay free to use right here.

      

      

        How do I open the actual app?
        
The working app — your board, your listings, your sales — opens with a signed-in account, on your phone or your laptop, the same way on both. Request early access above and you'll get your way in by text or email. A public version where anyone can create their own account is the next build phase; this page is how you get in line for it.
